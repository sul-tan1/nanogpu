Instruction guide

Each pixel starts with r = x and b = y. The program updates r, and the final byte sets the brightness. Coordinates are absolute on the canvas.

NOP
Keep r
ADD / SUB
Add or subtract b, wrapping at 8 bits
XOR
Exclusive OR with b
MAX
Keep the larger unsigned byte
SHR1
Shift r right by one bit
MUL_SHIFT4
Multiply signed r and b, shift right four bits, and keep the low byte
